A few words from me

Hasini Garu, Nijanga cheppalante… ee message rayadaniki kuda chala alochinchanu. Endukante ippudu nenu cheppedi naa gurinchi kaadu, nee gurinchi undali. Nenu chesina prathi daniki, naa over-caring valla, naa maatala valla, naa messages valla, teliyakunda aina nee mental peace disturb ayyi unte… I am truly sorry. Ninnu baadha pettalani, pressure cheyyalani, nee life lo burden avvalani nenu eppudu anukoledu. Kaani naa intention kanna nee experience important. Nuvvu ibbandi paddav ante, daniki nenu nijanga kshaminchamani adugutunnanu. Anduke ippudu nenu nee decision ni respect chesthanu. Ika nenu neeku messages cheyyanu. Reels pampanu. Audios, videos, calls… emi cheyyanu. Nee space ni disturb cheyyanu. Nuvvu nannu gurinchi alochinchakunda, nee life ni nee istam vachinattu peaceful ga continue cheyyagalige space ni nenu isthanu. Kaani oka vishayam matram abaddham cheppalenu. Nenu ninnu marchipoyanu ani cheppalenu. Naa prema poindani cheppalenu. Ninnu vadilesanu ani kuda cheppalenu. Nenu ikkade untanu ani cheppadam kuda ninnu wait cheyyamani pressure cheyyadaniki kaadu. Naa hrudayam lo unna nijanni dachukolekapovadam anthe. Mana story ni nenu ela marchipogalanu? Oka yellow dress lo ninnu chusi naa manasulo modalaina aa feeling… Nenu rasina aa letter… Mundhu nuvvu “interest ledu” ani cheppina aa rojulu… Tarvatha manam okariki okaram daggaraina rojulu… Mana madhya jarigina chinna chinna conversations… Nuvvu “ninnu vadili vellanu” ani cheppina aa maata… adi aa samayam lo nijam kaakapoyina, naa jeevithamlo adi nijamaina oka gnapakam. Manam kalisi Marvel movies chudali ani anukunna aa chinna dream kuda naaku chala pedda memory. Ninnu preminchadam lo nenu konni sarlu haddulu daatesi undochu. Ninnu ekkuvaga care cheyyalani try chesthu, ade care ninnu suffocate chesundachu. Ippudu adi ardham chesukuntunnanu. Anduke ee sari naa prema ni prove cheyyadaniki ninnu chase cheyyanu. Ninnu preminchadam ante nee daggaraki raavadam matrame kaadu… Nee manasuki avasaramaina dooram kuda ivvadam ani ippudu nerchukuntunnanu. Nuvvu eppudaina venakki tirigi chusthe, nenu ninnu force chesina vyakthiga kaakunda, tappulu chesina… kaani nijanga ninnu preminchina oka manishiga gurthundali ani korukuntunnanu. Nee life lo nenu undala vaddha anedi nee decision. Nuvvu malli naa daggaraki raavala vaddha anedi kuda nee decision. Nenu aa decision ni respect chesthanu. Kaani naa hrudayam matram oka chinna nammakanni dachukuntundi— Konni bandhalaki dooram anedi mugimpu kaadu… konchem samayam. Konni premalaki maatladakapovadam marupu kaadu… oka nishabdha eduruchoopu. Nuvvu eppudaina nijanga naa daggaraki tirigi raavalani anukunte, nenu emi adaganu—“enduku vachav?” ani kuda adaganu. Nuvvu vachav ante chaalu. Appudu manam mana kathani malli modalu pettalsina avasaram ledu. Manam ekkada aapesamo akkadinunchi oka sari nijanga maatladukundam. Until then… I will respect your silence. I will respect your space. I will respect your decision. And most importantly, I will never intentionally disturb your peace again. Ninnu vadileyadam naa chethilo undochu… kaani ninnu preminchadam naa manasulo jarigina vishayam. Adi nenu evarini oppinchadaniki kaadu, neepai hakku kosam kaadu. Adi just… naa nijam. Take care, Hasini. Nuvvu santhoshanga undali. Nuvvu peaceful ga undali. Naa jeevithamlo nuvvu entha importanto, adi nee daggaraki cheri cheppakunda kuda nenu mosukellagalanu. I am sorry. I love you. And now, I will give you the silence you asked for. Migatha antha… nee ishtam. ❤️

— Yours, always
